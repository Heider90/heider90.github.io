I am an economist at the [Austrian Institute of Economic Research (WIFO)](https://www.wifo.ac.at/) in Vienna and a Ph.D. candidate at the [Vienna University of Economics and Business (WU)](https://www.wu.ac.at/), where I also teach.

My research is on international trade and trade policy, economic sanctions, and geoeconomics. I study how sanctions regimes shape trade and macroeconomic outcomes across countries and sectors, working mainly with structural gravity (PPML) models and high-dimensional fixed-effects estimation on dyadic panel data.

A large part of my work is building the empirical infrastructure that policy research depends on. I am a core member of the [Global Sanctions Data Base (GSDB)](https://www.globalsanctionsdatabase.com/) team — an open international panel dataset used by researchers, governments, and financial institutions — where I contribute to primary data collection, sanctions coding, codebook documentation, and the reproducible pipeline behind Releases 4 and 5. I am also a member of the Research Centre International Economics (FIW).

From January to May 2026 I was a visiting researcher at the School of Economics, Drexel University, in Philadelphia.

Alongside my research I teach the undergraduate course *Databases and Methods of Empirical Economic Research* at WU, covering relational databases, SQL, and applied empirical workflows.

My work has appeared in *The World Economy* and *Perspektiven der Wirtschaftspolitik*, as well as in policy reports for wiiw and the Austrian National Bank. I also write for wider audiences, including CEPR/VoxEU, the *Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung*, and *profil*.

Research interests
------
* International trade and trade policy
* Economic sanctions and geoeconomics
* Applied econometrics: structural gravity estimation, dyadic panel data, high-dimensional fixed effects
* Empirical data infrastructure for policy research
